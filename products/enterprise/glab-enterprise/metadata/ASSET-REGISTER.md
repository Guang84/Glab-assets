## Status values

- `draft` — work in progress.
- `review` — awaiting review.
- `approved` — current official asset.
- `superseded` — replaced by a newer approved version.
- `archived` — retained for history.

Do not mark an asset approved until artwork, export, usage rights, and relevant checks have been reviewed.
