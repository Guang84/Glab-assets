# Identity

Canonical GLab Enterprise identity materials.

- `logos/` — approved corporate logo exports.
- `qr-codes/` — QR artwork and verified destination records.
- `brand-guidelines/` — logo clear space, minimum size, backgrounds, and do/don't examples.
- `color-system/` — approved HEX, RGB, CMYK, and design-token definitions.

Do not publish an unapproved logo variation as an official mark. Preserve the original GLab symbol artwork.
