# Enterprise Assets

This directory contains GLab Enterprise's corporate asset collection and the starter structure for future enterprise offerings.

## Collections

- [glab-enterprise/](glab-enterprise/README.md) — active corporate identity, stationery, digital assets, marketing, source artwork, and asset records for GLab Enterprise.
- [_template/](../enterprise/_template/README.md) — copy and rename this starter for a distinct future enterprise product, service, or module.

## Organization rules

1. Keep GLab Enterprise corporate assets in `glab-enterprise/`, not in the generic template.
2. Keep approved exports in their relevant category and editable masters in `source/`.
3. Keep drafts and retired files in `archive/`; do not mix them with approved production artwork.
4. Record purpose, status, version, source/creator, and rights information in the asset register.
5. Do not store confidential customer documents, credentials, or unnecessary personal data.
6. Preserve the repository's Brand Assets License and all third-party license terms.
